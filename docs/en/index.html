Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the quarantine process take?
The duration varies depending on the destination country, but generally ranges between 15 and 30 days. During this period, all veterinary tests and vaccinations required by the health authorities of the receiving country are performed.
Who accompanies the horses during the flight?
Our professional grooms accompany the horses throughout the entire flight. They are trained to attend to any need of the animal and ensure their well-being during the air journey.
What does the export quote include?
The quote includes: land transport from origin, quarantine and veterinary care, complete document management, air freight, and delivery at destination. Transport insurance is optional.
Is it mandatory to purchase insurance?
It is not mandatory, but we strongly recommend it. We offer advice and policy management through the most recognized insurers in the sector to give you complete peace of mind.
How do horses travel on the plane?
Horses travel in specially designed flight stalls that are loaded onto cargo planes. These stalls ensure space, ventilation, and safety, and professional grooms travel alongside them at all times.
How far in advance should I plan?
We recommend contacting us at least 2 months before the desired shipping date, to allow time to plan the quarantine and manage all documentation with sufficient margin.
