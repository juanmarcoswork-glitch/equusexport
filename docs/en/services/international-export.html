At Equus Export we take care of the entire export process for your horse, from pickup at your stable to delivery at the final destination. You just tell us where you want to send your horse; we take care of the rest.

Our door-to-door service covers every phase of the journey: quarantine, veterinary tests, health documentation, land transport, air freight and final delivery. All coordinated by a single team, with no intermediaries and no surprises.

It does not matter whether your horse is a sport horse, a breeder, a leisure horse or a companion animal. Every horse receives the same level of professional attention and care throughout the entire journey.
