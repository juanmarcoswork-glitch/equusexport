1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They are used to make the website function properly, to improve the user experience and to provide information to the website owners.

3. How to Manage Cookies?

You can configure your browser to accept or reject cookies. Below is how to do so in the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Please note that if you disable cookies, some website features may not be available.

4. Consent

When you first access this website, an information banner about the use of cookies will be displayed. If you continue browsing or expressly accept, we understand that you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

You may revoke your consent at any time by changing your browser settings or contacting us at info@equus-export.com.

5. Updates

This cookie policy may be updated periodically. We recommend reviewing it regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.
