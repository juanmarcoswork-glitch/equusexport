Process

Quarantine Protocol

To ensure that horses meet the health requirements demanded by the importing country and Spanish regulations for export, through a controlled isolation period, veterinary surveillance and official sample collection.

Phase 1

Intake and Initial Assessment

1

Identification
  • Microchip verification
  • Equine passport check
2

Initial Clinical Examination
  • Temperature, mucous membranes, auscultation, general condition
  • Confirmation of absence of signs of infectious-contagious diseases
3

Registration
  • Individual box assignment
  • Entry date and time recording
  • Official notification to the competent authority
4

Diagnostic Tests

Tests depend on the importing country. They usually include:

  • Equine Infectious Anemia
  • Contagious Equine Metritis (cultures)
  • Piroplasmosis (Babesia caballi and Theileria equi)
  • Glanders, Dourine and Rhinopneumonitis

Samples taken by an official veterinarian and sent to MAPA-authorized laboratories.

5

Vaccinations and Treatments
  • Vaccination against Equine Influenza and/or Rhinopneumonitis
  • Internal and external deworming
  • Preventive treatments against vector-borne diseases

Everything is recorded in the export file.

6

Observation Period
  • Daily temperature and clinical sign monitoring with records
  • No contact with external animals allowed
  • Daily cleaning and disinfection of boxes and corridors
  • Dedicated staff for the quarantine batch
Phase 2

Diagnosis and Treatment

Phase 3

Certification and Departure

7

Official Inspections

The MAPA official veterinarian conducts periodic inspections throughout the quarantine period.

8

Quarantine Completion
  • All laboratory results negative
  • No suspicious clinical signs
  • The official veterinarian issues the International Health Certificate (IHC)
  • Coordination of transport to the airport or departure point
9

Transport and Boarding
  • Authorized and disinfected vehicle
  • Complete documentation: passports, IHC, laboratory results
  • Official supervision until boarding
