Professional Fleet

Modern Fleet for Maximum Comfort

At Equus Export we have a modern and carefully equipped fleet to guarantee the wellbeing of every horse during transport. Each vehicle has been designed with equine comfort and safety in mind, incorporating the latest industry technology.

Our commitment to excellence drives us to maintain every unit in optimal condition, with periodic inspections and thorough maintenance that ensures reliability on every journey, whether national or international.
